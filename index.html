Rise & Bias Factory

An internal production tool for the Rise & Bias YouTube channel (@RiseAndBias).

Privacy PolicyTerms of Use

What this tool is

Rise & Bias Factory is a command-line tool that helps the owner of the Rise & Bias channel produce and upload English-language educational videos about business history and behavioral psychology.

  • It is used by one person only: the owner of the Rise & Bias channel.
  • It runs on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service, and there is no sign-up.
  • It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not access other people's accounts, channels, videos or data.

How it uses YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. After the owner signs in with Google (OAuth 2.0) and approves a finished video, the tool uploads it to the owner's channel as a private video with a scheduled publish time, and YouTube publishes it at that time. Each day this is at most one long video and up to five Shorts cut from it.

API methodPurpose
channels.list (mine)Confirm that the signed-in channel is the Rise & Bias channel before anything is uploaded.
videos.insertUpload the approved long video and its Shorts as private videos with a scheduled publish time, with the altered or synthetic content setting (containsSyntheticMedia) set to true and "made for kids" set to no.
thumbnails.setSet the thumbnail the owner chose for the long video.
captions.insertUpload the English caption file for the long video.
videos.listRead back the status of the uploaded video, and re-check that previously uploaded videos still exist (see the Privacy Policy).

OAuth scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl (required by captions.insert; it also covers the other methods above).

Human review

  • Every script is checked against its sources and approved by the owner before production continues.
  • Every finished video and thumbnail is approved by the owner before it can be uploaded. The owner chooses the title.
  • Before anything is sent, the tool shows the YouTube upload notice below and uploads only when the owner confirms it. A dry-run mode shows exactly what would be sent without connecting to YouTube.
  • Each upload is started by the owner with an explicit command. The tool does not upload unattended.
  • AI-generated narration and images are disclosed in the video settings and in the description.
